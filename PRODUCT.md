# ScoutPath — product context

**Register:** product (a learning library and personal tracker). The home page leans brand, every other surface is app UI.

**Who and where.** A 12–17-year-old Scout at a kitchen table on a school night, a parent helping on a phone, or a merit badge counselor at a troop meeting under fluorescent light. They come with one requirement in mind ("First Aid 7", "Tenderfoot 4a") and want to know what it asks, what to read, and what they still have to do. Calm, focused, trustworthy — never gamified.

**What it must always be honest about.** ScoutPath is independent. It never reproduces official requirement wording, labels every outline with how far it was verified, never invents numbering, and never implies a personal progress mark is an official sign-off.

**Jobs.** Find a requirement fast (search, A–Z, Eagle path, rank trail). Understand it (summary, kind of work, orientation, safety). Learn from curated public resources. Practice (flashcards, quizzes). Plan and track privately in the browser.

**Anti-goals.** No accounts, analytics, leaderboards, streak pressure, or social features. No decorative dashboards.
